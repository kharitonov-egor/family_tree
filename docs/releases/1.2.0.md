# Family Tree 1.2.0

## Horse breeding stats

Family Tree records speed, jump height, and health for horses, donkeys, mules, llamas, trader llamas, camels, skeleton horses, and zombie horses. Speed is in blocks per second, jump in blocks, and health in hearts, using the Minecraft Wiki's conversions. Llamas also show strength and their chest slots.

- Tree cards show each animal's stats. A green ▲ marks a stat higher than both parents, and a red ▼ marks one lower than both.
- Select a pet in the tree to compare each stat with both parents' values. Small windows and large GUI scales show a one-line summary instead.
- **Sort** in the browser orders pets by speed, jump, or health. Pets without stats follow in name order.
- Exported PNGs include the stats and a legend for the markers.
- Settings has **Show horse stats** to hide the stats everywhere, including exports.
- `/familytree info <name>` lists the stats.
- Horses, donkeys, mules, skeleton horses, and zombie horses have portraits. In the game they match coat and markings; exports show a side profile cut from the animal's texture. Llamas and camels have in-game portraits only.

Stats are base values, so potion effects don't change them. Family Tree reads them when it records an animal and again whenever the server loads, unloads, or scans it.

## Camels and skeleton trap horses

Minecraft treats every camel as tamed, and skeleton trap horses are tamed without an owner. Version 1.1.0 recorded each of these animals as soon as it loaded, without an owner, so only operators could see them. Now Family Tree records them once a player rides or breeds them, and that player becomes their owner in Family Tree. Minecraft's own ownership stays unchanged. Wild camels nobody rides stay out of the tree. Ownerless records that 1.1.0 already saved stay in the history, and the first player to ride one of those animals becomes its owner.

Foals that are not tamed yet keep their last known location and stats current, because their record already exists from breeding.

## Upgrade

Update the server and clients together. This release changes the snapshot, open, discovery, and rename channels, and NeoForge registers protocol 3.

Saved records gain one optional `horse_stats` field. Saves from 1.1.0 load unchanged, and horses recorded by 1.1.0 get stats the next time they load. If you go back to 1.1.0, it keeps every record but drops the stats on its next save. They return when the horses load under 1.2.0.
